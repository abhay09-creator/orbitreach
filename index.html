FAQs

Good questions. Clear answers.

Here are a few things to consider when exploring satellite connectivity for your site.

Satellite internet connects a site to the internet through a satellite network and a terminal installed at the location. The exact service, equipment and performance depend on the provider and plan.
It can be a useful option for some locations where fibre or mobile broadband is unavailable or unreliable. Feasibility and service availability need to be checked for each site.
Speed, latency and data policies vary by provider, technology, plan, network conditions and location. We recommend assessing your actual applications and required performance before selecting a service.
Costs depend on the provider, equipment, installation, plan and site requirements. Share your location and use case for a more relevant discussion; this site does not publish unverified prices.
Timing depends on provider availability, equipment, site access, installation conditions and any approvals needed. A site-specific review is the best way to estimate the timeline.
No blanket coverage guarantee is made. Availability and regulatory requirements vary, so each location should be checked with the relevant service provider before any commitment.
